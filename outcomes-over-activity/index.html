ODM Principle

Outcomes Over Activity

Progress should be measured through value created rather than work completed.

By Ronnie Yates6 min readOutcome-Driven Management
All articles

Activity Is Visible

Tasks, milestones, utilization, and delivery are easy to see. They describe motion, but they do not prove that customer behavior, business performance, or operational capability improved.

Outcomes Create Direction

An outcome describes meaningful change. It allows teams to adapt the solution while retaining clarity about success.

Keep Activity as Evidence

Activity metrics remain useful when they help explain outcome movement, expose risk, or inform a decision. They become harmful when completion becomes the definition of success.

The purpose of management information is better action and better decisions.

The Outcome-Driven Management Manifesto

We are discovering better ways to lead organizations through outcomes, evidence, learning, and adaptation.

Through this work, we have come to value:

01

Outcomes over Activity

Measure meaningful change and value created, not simply effort or work completed.

02

Evidence over Opinion

Use reality and actionable signals to guide the next move.

03

Learning over Certainty

Make assumptions visible, testable, and revisable.

04

Adaptation over Rigidity

Change the path when evidence reveals a better way to the outcome.

05

Signals over Reporting

Prioritize clarity and meaning over information volume.

06

Decision Quality over Process Compliance

Design management systems to enable action, not ceremonial adherence.

07

Customer Value over Output Volume

Judge success by customer and business impact rather than production alone.

08

Transparency over Visibility

Create shared understanding of priorities, commitments, dependencies, evidence, and risk.

The items on the right retain value.

In uncertain environments, we value the items on the left more.

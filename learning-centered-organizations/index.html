Why learning matters

The path is increasingly discovered, not prescribed.

In complex and AI-enabled work, the original problem may be incomplete, the best intervention may be unknown, and customer response may only become visible through experimentation. More planning does not automatically remove this uncertainty.

A Learning-Centered Organization preserves direction through outcomes while treating the path as something to test, observe, and improve.

The organization does not abandon execution. The organization makes execution produce learning.
